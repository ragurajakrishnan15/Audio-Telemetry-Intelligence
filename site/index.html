Limitations of this project
  • The data is synthetic. Relationships were written into the generator, so scores show that the pipeline works, not that the patterns exist in real telemetry.
  • The ANC predictor is largely circular. Its target is generated from ambient noise, and 97% of the model's importance sits on that one feature. The R² of 0.886 measures recovery of the generator's formula.
  • The duration forecaster does not work. R² ≈ 0 by design of the data. It's kept as an honest negative result.
  • Segmentation is weak. Silhouette is 0.17. ANC usage is the only strong separator.
