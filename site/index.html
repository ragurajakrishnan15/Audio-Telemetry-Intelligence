Pipeline Limitations
  • Synthetic Foundation: Since associations were formulated natively by the generator, analytical results prove the mechanics, not physical real-world facts.
  • Circular ANC Prediction: The target algorithm was generated from ambient noise. The R² rating largely replicates the generator formulas instead of modeling real hardware performance.
  • Duration Forcasting Bounds: Session duration was randomized; forecasting is impossible by mathematical design. Kept active for experimental integrity.
  • Silhouette Weakness: Score tracked at ~0.17— indicating heavy overlap between Standard and High-Activity users beside spatial features.
